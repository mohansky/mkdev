Spatialedge Technologies is a geospatial, software and infrastructure solutions company based in Mysuru, India. They work with government and enterprise clients on everything from survey data and enterprise GIS to GeoAI and digital twins.

Built with Astro and TailwindCSS and hosted on Cloudflare, the website presents a broad and technical offering in a calm, precise way. The design uses a restrained navy and teal palette, generous white space and a subtle grid motif that echoes the company's mapping work.

Key project features:
- Clear content structure across Services, Solutions and Industries pages
- Clean, typography-led design with a restrained colour palette
- Structured data and SEO foundations for better search visibility
- Self-hosted fonts and static pages for fast loading times
- Spam-protected contact form for project enquiries

The result is a confident and credible web presence that helps a specialised engineering company explain what it does to decision-makers in government and enterprise.
