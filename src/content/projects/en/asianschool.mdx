The Asian School is a private CBSE school in Tubli, Bahrain. It opened in 1983 and today teaches around 4,600 students from Kindergarten to Grade 12.

A school of this size publishes a lot: circulars, newsletters, events, achievements and admission information. The website was built with Next.js and Bootstrap, with Contentful as the headless CMS, so that the school's own staff can keep everything up to date without touching any code.

Key project features:
- Headless CMS so staff can publish news, circulars and events themselves
- School calendar, newsletters and a photo gallery in one Updates section
- Admission procedure, fees and curriculum laid out clearly for new parents
- Homepage slideshow that shows off the campus
- Responsive design that works well on phones and desktops

The result is a website that works as the school's daily noticeboard for parents and students, and as a first introduction for new families.
