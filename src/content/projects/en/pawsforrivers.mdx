Paws for Rivers makes high-protein, eco-friendly pet food in India from invasive fish species. Every meal feeds a pet and helps native freshwater fish at the same time.

It is a serious mission with a lot of humour, and the website had to carry both. Built with Astro, TailwindCSS and GSAP animations, it is playful and colourful, with cut-out pets, a wave-patterned backdrop and small animated details that make the story fun to scroll through.

Key project features:
- Playful, colourful design with animated details
- Shop page with the product range and a cart
- Simple pre-order flow for pet owners
- Dedicated page on the sustainability goals behind the brand
- Fast, static pages that load quickly on mobile

The result is a website with as much personality as the brand itself, which explains an unusual idea in a way people remember.
