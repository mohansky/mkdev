La Volotte is a charming little wool shop in Germany with a carefully chosen range of high-quality natural yarns from different countries. Alongside the yarn, the shop runs knitting and felting workshops and a monthly knitting café.

The website brings that small-shop feeling online. It was built with Hugo and Bootstrap, with Snipcart for the shopping cart, which keeps the shop simple to run: adding a new yarn is as easy as adding a new product entry.

Key project features:
- Online shop with cart and checkout
- Simple product listings that make frequent updates easy
- News section for workshops and the knitting café
- FAQ page that answers the common questions about ordering
- Cookie consent that keeps statistics optional

The result is a friendly online shop that reflects the character of the store and lets customers order their favourite yarns from anywhere.
