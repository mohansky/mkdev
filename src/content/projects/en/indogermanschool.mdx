Indo-German School is a preschool in Kasturi Nagar, Bengaluru, offering Playgroup, Nursery, LKG and UKG programmes for children aged 2 to 6.

The project is two things in one: a warm, welcoming website for parents, and a school management app for the staff behind it. It was built with TanStack Start, React and TailwindCSS, and runs on Cloudflare Workers. The chalkboard-inspired design, hand-drawn doodles and friendly serif headlines give the site a playful character while keeping the important information easy to find.

Key project features:
- Admissions enquiries and contact messages collected in one place for the school
- Photo gallery, blog and FAQ section for parents
- Staff dashboard for students, attendance, fees and the school calendar
- Secure sign-in with role-based access for admins and staff
- Light and dark colour schemes with system preference detection

The result is a single platform that helps parents get to know the school and helps the school run its day-to-day work.
