Sapphire Skin & Aesthetics Clinic is a dermatology and aesthetics clinic with two branches in Bengaluru, India. It offers more than 30 treatments, from laser hair reduction and chemical peels to hair transplants.

Most visitors arrive with one question: can this clinic help with my concern, and how do I book? The website was built with Astro, TailwindCSS and DaisyUI to answer that quickly, with a clear treatments catalogue and a booking button that is always within reach.

Key project features:
- Treatments catalogue with a page for each treatment
- Appointment booking form with a callback option
- Before-and-after gallery and patient reviews
- Blog and FAQ section for common skin and hair questions
- Contact details for both branches, including WhatsApp

The result is a bright, reassuring website that helps patients find the right treatment and makes booking an appointment easy.
